Elena Kirshanova

Lead Cryptographer, Technology Innovation Institute

I work on practical and theoretical cryptanalysis of lattice-based assumptions, and on quantum algorithms that speed up attacks on lattice- and code-based schemes.

About

I received my PhD from Ruhr University Bochum at the Chair of Cryptology and IT Security, advised by Alexander May. I was then a postdoc at ENS Lyon in the AriC team, working with Damien Stehlé.
